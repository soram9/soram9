## 🧠 PROJECT ALETHEIA

### A Private, Local-First Artificial Intelligence System

I'm building **Project Aletheia**, a long-term initiative to create a persistent, autonomous, privacy-first personal AI system that can reason, learn, remember, and participate in its own development under human governance.

Not another chatbot. Not another subscription-dependent assistant.

The vision is an intelligent system that operates on privately controlled infrastructure, preserves its knowledge across generations of hardware and AI models, and becomes progressively more capable without sacrificing transparency, security, or human authority.

### 🔑 Guiding Principles

- 🔒 **Privacy by Design** — Personal data stays under the owner's control.
- 🏠 **Local First** — Prioritize privately operated infrastructure and offline-capable AI.
- 🧠 **Persistent Intelligence** — Build durable memory, reasoning, and contextual continuity.
- 🤖 **Governed Autonomy** — AI should plan, execute, verify, and improve within explicit boundaries.
- 📚 **Continuous Learning** — Turn experience into structured, auditable knowledge.
- 🔍 **Truth Over Assumption** — Separate verified facts from inference and speculation.
- 🧩 **Modular Architecture** — Replace models, hardware, and components without losing knowledge.
- 🛠️ **AI Building AI** — Develop the ability to maintain, test, and improve its own software under human oversight.

### ⚙️ Technology & Development

Areas planned for exploration and evaluation include:

- Local large language models and adaptive AI orchestration
- Python, Linux, and open-source infrastructure
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and persistent memory
- Vector databases and structured knowledge systems
- Natural voice interaction and conversational continuity
- Autonomous agents and AI-assisted software engineering
- Machine-checkable verification and governed self-maintenance
- Privacy-first automation and modular system integration

**Development Status:** Long-term development initiative. The capabilities above describe the intended direction; their implementation and validation are not established by this profile.
